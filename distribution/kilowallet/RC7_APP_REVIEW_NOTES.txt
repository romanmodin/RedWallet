Kilowallet 1.0.0 (30) is a self-custody wallet for the Bitcoin BLAKE2b (XBT) chain. Its on-chain balance is separate from SHA-256 Bitcoin. No account or sign-in is required.

Review with an empty wallet: create a new wallet on the test device, open Receive to inspect its address and QR code, then open Settings > BTC node & explorer. No funds, existing recovery words, node credentials or transaction are needed for this walkthrough.

This build fixes an iOS Lightning engine call used by payments, channel operations and invoice updates. It adds an optional connection to a compatible BLAKE2b node. Users may scan or enter RPC connection details and compare an HTTPS certificate fingerprint before sharing the login. RPC credentials are stored in the device Keychain. When enabled, the node receives public receive/change descriptors for a watch-only wallet; this lets the node associate addresses and history. The import does not send private spending keys. Kilojoin and JoinBlaket still use Electrum.

The optional Lightning bridge quotes XBT payments to SHA-256 Lightning invoices. Collaborative rounds require the app to stay open; iOS permits only limited background execution. Wallet backups are user-directed and can contain recovery material. These flows are optional and no payment is required for review.
